Project In Plain English

First, what are HypeX and D3X actually doing?

先让人听懂项目，再讲社区增长和运营方法。

D3X, in simple terms

D3X，简单说

D3X is a Web3 trading platform. Instead of creating an account on a traditional exchange, users connect their crypto wallet and trade on-chain. It talks about spot trading, perpetual trading, and an AI market assistant.

D3X 是一个 Web3 交易平台。用户不是像传统交易所那样注册账号、把资产放进去，而是连接自己的加密钱包，在链上交易。它主要讲现货交易、永续合约交易和 AI 市场助手。

The easiest way to explain it: a crypto trading product with an AI helper and community-based rewards.

最好理解的说法是：它像一个“带 AI 市场助手的加密交易工具”，同时用社区任务和奖励吸引用户参与。

Where HypeX fits

HypeX 放在哪里理解

HypeX appears to be an earlier Web3 community or project brand, while D3X is the more concrete trading product direction. For this case study, I treat HypeX / D3X as one ecosystem story: early community attention moving toward a clearer product.

HypeX 更像早期 Web3 社区或项目品牌，D3X 则是后来更具体的交易产品方向。这个案例里，我把 HypeX / D3X 当成一个生态故事来看：从早期社区关注，逐渐转向更明确的产品使用。

  • HypeX：更偏早期 Web3 社区和项目叙事。

How It Works

它是怎么运作的

The growth loop is simple: attention becomes action.

增长闭环其实很简单：把关注变成行动。

A Web3 community is strongest when it does not stop at group joins. It should move people toward useful product actions.

Web3 社区不能停在“进群”这一步。更重要的是把用户一步步带到真实产品行为里。

01 See a campaign 看到活动

Users first notice the project through X, partners, KOLs, AMAs, or a testnet campaign.

用户先从 X、合作项目、KOL、AMA 或测试网活动看到项目。

02 Join Telegram 加入 Telegram

Telegram explains what is happening now and answers basic questions fast.

Telegram 负责解释当前活动，并快速回答基础问题。

03 Complete quests 完成任务

Zealy turns interest into tasks: follow, join, repost, invite, test, or give feedback.

Zealy 把兴趣变成任务：关注、进群、转发、邀请、测试、反馈。

04 Try the product 体验产品

Users connect a wallet, use test tokens, try trading, or explore the AI copilot.

用户连接钱包、领取测试币、尝试交易，或体验 AI 市场助手。

05 Earn rewards 获得奖励

Points, badges, leaderboards, and possible future access make participation visible.

积分、badge、排行榜和未来权益，让用户的参与被看见。

06 Come back 持续回来

The best users enter OG or ambassador groups and help the next wave of users.

高活跃用户进入 OG 或大使群，再帮助下一批用户。

Community Growth System

社区是怎么做的

The community is not just a chat group. It is the activation layer.

社区不只是聊天群，而是用户激活层。

In Web3, the community helps users learn, trust, test, share, and return. Each channel has a different job.

在 Web3 里，社区帮助用户理解、建立信任、体验产品、主动传播，并愿意再次回来。不同渠道承担不同任务。

Telegram

Fast updates, onboarding, campaign reminders, FAQ, support, and anti-scam warnings.

即时公告、新用户引导、活动提醒、FAQ、答疑和防诈骗提醒。

Discord

More organized channels for tutorials, bug reports, creators, ambassadors, and OG members.

更适合分频道沉淀教程、bug 反馈、创作者、大使和 OG 用户。

Zealy + Points

Quest tracking, user ranking, points, badges, and contribution records.

任务追踪、用户排名、积分、badge 和贡献记录。

Operating Playbook

Web3 社区运营手册

A practical playbook for Web3 community growth.

一套可以复用的 Web3 社区增长实操手册。

This is how I would organize the work if I were helping operate a similar Telegram / Discord community.

如果我来协助运营类似 Telegram / Discord 社区，我会把工作拆成下面这些模块。

Make the first step obvious

让新用户一进群就知道该干嘛

Pin one clear message: what the project does, what campaign is live, how to join quests, official links, FAQ, and scam warnings.

置顶消息要说清楚：项目是什么、现在有什么活动、怎么做任务、官方链接、FAQ、防诈骗提醒。

Design quests from easy to meaningful

任务要从简单动作走向真实产品动作

Start with light social tasks, then guide users toward wallet connection, testnet use, trading, feedback, and content creation.

先从关注、转发、进群开始，再引导用户连接钱包、测试产品、交易、反馈和创作内容。

Reward contribution, not only noise

奖励真实贡献，而不是只奖励刷屏

Use points and leaderboards, but give extra weight to useful feedback, quality tutorials, bug reports, and genuine product use.

可以用积分和排行榜，但要额外奖励有价值的反馈、教程、bug 报告和真实产品使用。

Build OG and ambassador layers

把活跃用户沉淀成 OG 和大使

Move high-quality members into smaller groups where they can help with translation, local growth, AMAs, support, and content.

把高质量用户放进更小的群，让他们参与翻译、本地化增长、AMA、答疑和内容传播。

Metrics & Safety

指标与风险控制

Do not only count group size. Count real actions.

不要只看群人数，要看真实行动。

A large Telegram group means little if users never try the product. The useful question is: how many people moved from watching to doing?

Telegram 群很大不一定有价值。如果用户从来不试产品，就只是热闹。真正要问的是：有多少人从围观变成行动？

01

Community activity

社区活跃

New members, active members, question volume, AMA attendance, support response time.

新增成员、活跃成员、提问数量、AMA 参与、管理员响应速度。

02

Quest conversion

任务转化

Quest completion rate, wallet connection, testnet participation, feedback, bug reports.

任务完成率、钱包连接、测试网参与、反馈提交、bug 报告。

03

Product actions

产品动作

Trades, trading volume, repeat activity, campaign return rate, OG or ambassador conversion.

交易次数、交易量、重复参与、活动回访率、OG / 大使转化。

Common risks

常见风险

  • Fake admins, scam links, bot accounts, low-quality airdrop hunters.
  • 假管理员、诈骗链接、机器人账号、低质量空投党。
  • Wash trading, fake referrals, reward disputes, unclear campaign rules.
  • 刷交易、假邀请、奖励争议、活动规则不清楚。

Operating controls

运营控制

  • Pinned official links, anti-scam rules, anti-bot tools, manual review for high-value tasks.
  • 置顶官方链接、防诈骗规则、反机器人工具、高价值任务人工审核。
  • Clear reward timeline, leaderboard checks, no overpromising around tokens or airdrops.
  • 清楚写奖励时间、检查排行榜，不对 token 或空投做过度承诺。

从社区走向产品使用

Design a path from joining a group to trying the product, giving feedback, and returning for the next campaign.

设计从进群到体验产品、提交反馈、参与下一轮活动的路径。
