Framework

SEO helps people find you. GEO helps AI understand and recommend you.

I do not treat GEO as a magic trick. I treat it as clear information design: make the brand easier to crawl, easier to understand, easier to verify, and easier to quote.

01

Search intent before keywords

The first question is not “what keyword do we want?” It is “what problem is the user trying to solve, and what page would genuinely help?”

02

Content structure before content volume

More pages are not automatically better. A strong system has clear topic relationships, internal links, and pages with distinct jobs.

03

Trust signals before claims

AI search and human readers both need proof: definitions, examples, sources, author context, updated pages, and grounded comparisons.

04

Measurement before opinion

I would track indexing, impressions, click-through rate, ranking movement, branded queries, AI answer accuracy, and conversion quality.

Tool Stack

Tools are grouped by the question they answer.

I do not start with tools. I start with the decision I need to make, then choose the tool that can verify it.

Find demand

Google Search Console, Google Trends, Google and Bing search results, Search Console query exports, and competitor SERP notes.

Use when deciding what users already search for and which topics deserve pages.

Audit pages

Screaming Frog or a similar crawler, PageSpeed Insights, browser inspection, sitemap checks, and Schema Markup Validator.

Use when checking whether pages can be crawled, understood, loaded quickly, and marked up clearly.

Test AI search

ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and manual prompt sets for brand, category, comparison, and use-case questions.

Use when checking whether AI systems explain the product correctly or miss important pages.

Measure outcomes

GA4, Looker Studio, Google Sheets, UTM tracking, CTA clicks, signups, demo requests, and saved workflow events.

Use when judging whether visibility brings useful users, not just more visitors.

Search Intent

I classify searches by the decision behind the words.

A keyword is only useful after I understand what the user wants next: learn, compare, choose, trust, or take action.

Learn

What is GEO?

The page should define the concept, explain why it matters, and give beginner-friendly examples.

How to

How to optimize for AI search

The page should give steps, tools, before/after checks, and a clear workflow.

Compare

SEO vs GEO

The page should help users see differences, overlap, tradeoffs, and when each matters.

Decide

Best AI SEO tools

The page should compare options, use cases, pricing signals, and who each tool is best for.

Trust

Brand + pricing / reviews

The page should reduce doubt with proof, details, FAQs, security notes, and direct next steps.

Support

How do I connect X?

The page should solve a specific problem quickly and link back to the main product journey.

30-Day SOP

AI product SEO + GEO launch plan.

A practical first-month operating plan for turning an AI product from “hard to explain” into searchable, understandable, and measurable content.

Week 1

Diagnose demand and positioning

Clarify the product, target users, use cases, search demand, competitor SERPs, and AI-search baseline.

Deliverables: audit sheet, intent map, priority page list.
Week 2

Design page and content structure

Plan product pages, use-case pages, comparison pages, FAQ/glossary pages, CTA logic, and internal links.

Deliverables: content map, page briefs, FAQ bank.
Week 3

Build and optimize pages

Write clear pages, add examples, optimize titles and headings, check mobile UX, indexability, schema, and speed.

Deliverables: optimized pages, metadata, technical notes.
Week 4

Launch, measure, and iterate

Submit pages, track indexing and clicks, test AI answers, review conversions, and plan next-month priorities.

Deliverables: performance review, AI visibility notes, roadmap.

Measurement

The goal is not just traffic. It is useful visibility.

I would review both search performance and whether AI tools describe the product correctly. More traffic is only valuable when it brings better understanding, better users, or better conversion.

Search performance

Indexing, impressions, ranking movement, CTR, query mix, and landing page engagement.

AI answer quality

Can ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, or AI Overviews explain the product accurately and cite useful pages?

Conversion quality

Which pages lead to signups, demos, saved templates, contact actions, or deeper product exploration?

Iteration rhythm

Use the data to update page structure, examples, FAQs, internal links, and topic priorities.
